Warm Ups

I structure my warm ups based on the RAMP framework. This framework is not only used by many professional athletes, but there is also scientific evidence that it improves performance compared to static stretching. This page contains an explanation of the RAMP principles as well as three sample warm ups.

What Does RAMP Mean?

Raise

  • elevate heartrate, body temperature, respiration rate, and blood flow
  • eg: walking, jogging, active technique drills

Activate

  • activate key muscle groups
  • focus on stabilization
  • eg: band work, core exercises, balance drills

Mobilize

  • mobilize joints
  • focus on dynamic flexibility, not just static stretches
  • eg: ankle circle, shoulder circles, body weight squats/lunges

Potentiate

  • “primes” you for the workout
  • most explosive movements
  • eg: sprints, jumping
