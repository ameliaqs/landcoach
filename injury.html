Injury Prevention

According to a study of university dragon boat paddlers, the most common dragon boat injuries are to the lower back and shoulders. This page is meant to give you some exercises to prevent these common injuries through strengething your core, back, hips, and shoulders.

IMPORTANT: These are meant to be preventative; if you are feeling injured in any way, please visit the PT room in Wooden (1282D).

Low Back and Hips

These exercises focus on strengtheing the core, glutes, and hips to prevent low back and hip injuries. Strengthening these muscles increases stability and prevents your back from overcompensating, which can lead to injury.

Glute Bridge

  1. lie with you knees bent to a comfortable distance
  2. engage your core, pressing your low back into the ground
  3. press through your heels to slowly lift your hips off the ground: you should feel your core, glutes, and hamstrings engaged (imagine pulling your heels towards your butt)
  4. keeping your abs engaged, slowly lower your hips down

Variations: Single leg glute bridges, single leg glute bridge w/ rotation
