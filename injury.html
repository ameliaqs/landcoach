Injury Prevention

According to a study of university dragon boat paddlers, the most common dragon boat injuries are to the lower back and shoulders.

This page is meant to give you some exercises to prevent these common injuries through strengething your core, back, hips, and shoulders.

IMPORTANT: These are meant to be preventative; if you are feeling injured in any way, please visit the PT room in Wooden (1282D).
