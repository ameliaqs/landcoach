Cool Downs

While warm ups are all about raising your heart rate and activating your muscles, cool down is about gradually easing out of your workout and is a great time to focus on static stretches and improving flexibility!

A good cooldown doesn’t need to be long; 5-10 minutes is plenty. Here you will find exercises organized by muscle group. Hold each stretch for at least 30 seconds. You should feel every stretch but the pain should not be unbearable. If it’s so painful, your body will end up tensing up more, which is the opposite of what you want.
